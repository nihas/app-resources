Merge Garage is a vehicle merging game provided by **Nihas Nizar**. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the game.

## Gameplay information

The current game does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone access, or camera access. Its gameplay code does not send your scores, vehicle collection, or game progress to a developer-operated server.

The current game does not include advertising, in-app purchases, or social sign-in.

## Local data and retention

The game keeps active gameplay state on your device during a session. The current version does not provide a cloud save service or an account system. Quitting a game or restarting it discards the current session's progress.

You can remove app-local files and settings by clearing the application's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game. Copies retained by Android's backup services, if applicable, are controlled through your device and Google account settings.

## Unity and Google Play

The game is built with Unity and distributed through Google Play. Google Play and Android may process download, installation, security, and diagnostic information under their own settings and policies. Information handled by these platform services is subject to their respective privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)

## Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. The current gameplay code does not upload player information to a developer-operated backend or share it with advertising providers.

## Contacting us

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to your inquiry and retain it only as long as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of your correspondence, subject to those obligations.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in support messages.

## Children

The current game does not ask players to submit personal information or create an account. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## Security

We limit the personal information we request and use reasonable safeguards for support correspondence. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change, including if we add advertising, analytics, purchases, accounts, or cloud saves. The updated policy will show a revised date.
