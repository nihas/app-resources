# Privacy Policy for Merge Garage

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

> Draft: Before publication, resolve the Unity diagnostics review in Section 5 and PRIVACY_RELEASE_NOTES.md.

Merge Garage ("the game", "we", "our", or "us") is a toy vehicle merging game developed by Nihas Nizar. Players launch and combine matching vehicles to discover upgraded vehicles.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Merge Garage.

## 1. Information We Collect

You are not required to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location to play the game.

The current gameplay code does not transmit your scores, vehicle collection, or game progress to a developer-operated server. Information that may be processed by the Unity runtime is addressed in Section 5.

If you contact us for support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include in your message. We use this information to respond to your inquiry.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Merge Garage keeps active gameplay information, such as the vehicles in the current game, on your device during a session. The current version does not include an account system or cloud saves. Restarting the game or quitting to the main menu discards the current session's progress.

Any app-local files and settings can be removed by clearing the application's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game. Copies retained by Android's backup services, if applicable, are controlled through your device and Google account settings.

## 3. Advertising

The current version of Merge Garage does **not display advertisements** and does not include advertising functionality.

If advertisements are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the advertising services used and any information that may be processed by those services.

## 4. Analytics and Tracking

The current version does not include a separately integrated gameplay analytics service or advertising trackers intended to track users across apps or websites. Unity engine diagnostics are separate from these features and are addressed in Section 5.

If analytics or similar services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Merge Garage is built with Unity and distributed through Google Play. Google Play and Android may process installation, security, and diagnostic information under their own policies and settings.

**Release review required:** Unity engine diagnostics are enabled in the current project configuration. Before publishing this policy, confirm the release build's actual diagnostic data collection. Either disable and verify diagnostics, or replace this paragraph with a description of the data collected, its purpose, recipients, retention, and user controls. The absence of ads or accounts does not establish that Unity collects no data.

Relevant service policies:

- [Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 6. Data Sharing, Retention, and Deletion

We do not sell or rent personal information. The current gameplay code does not share player information with advertising providers or upload it to a developer-operated backend.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve your request or meet applicable legal obligations. You may email us to request deletion of your correspondence, subject to those obligations.

Local game information can be removed as described in Section 2. Data processed by platform or engine services is subject to the practices described in Section 5 and the applicable service policies.

## 7. Data Security

Merge Garage is designed to minimize the information requested from players. Active gameplay information is handled locally on the user's device. We use reasonable safeguards for support correspondence.

Please note that no device, software environment, or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The current game does not ask children or other players to submit personal information or create an account.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Merge Garage may receive new features and functionality over time.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game's functionality or data practices change, including if we introduce advertising, analytics, purchases, cloud saves, accounts, or other online services.

Changes will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last updated" date.
